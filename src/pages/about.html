About us

Supplements from a menopause clinic

Supplements by Hazel is a UK company run by Hazel Hayden, a Consultant Nurse who treats women and men at her clinics in Bristol. We make food supplements for the years before, during and after the menopause, and print every ingredient on the label.

How we make them

Two years of research

Hazel spent more than two years researching vitamins, minerals and botanical extracts before launching the range. Each supplement is made to sit alongside a balanced diet, and none of them contain fillers or bulking agents.

The health claims we make are the ones the UK and EU allow, and the full ingredient list is on every tub.

Meet Hazel · Founder

Meet Hazel Hayden

Thirty-eight years of looking after women and men in midlife.

Hazel Hayden has spent 38 years in the NHS, 19 of them in primary care. She is a Consultant Nurse, a British Menopause Society Registered Specialist and Trainer, and a clinical expert in women's and men's health. She teaches other healthcare professionals across the UK.

She started out as a specialist nurse in cardiovascular, metabolic and respiratory health. In 2006, after working as a specialist nurse in the commercial world and having had her two sons, she moved into women's health as well as continuing her previous specialisms: periods, perimenopause, menopause and healthy ageing. Men's health followed, covering testosterone, heart health, weight and lifestyle.

Hazel went on to found the Bristol Menopause Clinic and Bristol Health Hub, where women and men receive personal, evidence-based care. Supplements by Hazel comes from the same place: food supplements made to the standard she wanted for her own patients. She takes the ones she recommends herself.

She holds a Master's degree in Sexual Health in Menopause and a Diploma in Nutrition.

A personal mission

Why Hazel made her own

Going through the menopause herself, Hazel tried the supplements on sale and found many of them expensive, full of fillers and low in the ingredients that mattered.

She spent two years working out what she wanted in each formula. The result is four supplements, each for a different stage of life, with no additives or bulking agents.

They are food supplements, not medicines, and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
