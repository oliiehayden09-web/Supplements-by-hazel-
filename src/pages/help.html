Choosing a product

Which product is right for me?

Each formula is designed for a different stage of life:

  • Peri for pre-midlife, with ashwagandha, saffron and biotin
  • Clarity for everyday support through midlife, with magnesium, vitamin D, B12 and live cultures
  • Rosy for post-midlife, taken in the evening
  • Hair & Skin for hair, skin and nails at any stage
  • Perform, our formula for men

Our bundles bring three formulas together. If you're unsure, email {{email}} and we'll help.

Can I take more than one of your products together?

Our Wellbeing and Hormone Health bundles are designed to be taken together. If you'd like to combine products in another way, or you take other supplements, check the labels so you don't exceed recommended levels of any nutrient, and ask a healthcare professional if you're unsure.

Are your products vegan?

Clarity and Rosy are suitable for vegans. Peri and Perform are suitable for vegetarians. Hair & Skin contains marine collagen, so it isn't suitable for vegetarians or vegans.

All our products are free from artificial additives, gluten, soy and dairy.

Where are your products made?

Every product is made in the UK for Supplements by Hazel Limited, and packed in biodegradable packaging.

Taking our supplements

How should I take them?
  • Clarity: one capsule a day with a meal
  • Peri: one capsule twice a day with meals
  • Rosy: one capsule in the evening before bed
  • Hair & Skin: two capsules once a day, ideally with a meal
  • Perform: four capsules a day, two in the morning and two in the evening, with food

Always follow the directions on the label and don't exceed the recommended dose.

Can I take them with medication?

If you take prescription medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a medical condition, speak to your GP, pharmacist or another healthcare professional before starting any new supplement.

Are your supplements medicines?

No. Our products are food supplements. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and should not replace a varied, balanced diet and healthy lifestyle. The health claims we use are authorised under the EU/UK Nutrition and Health Claims Register.

Can Hazel give me personal advice?

Our customer team can help with questions about our products and orders. For personal clinical advice, Hazel and her colleagues see patients at the Bristol Menopause & Wellwoman Clinic and Bristol Health Hub.

Safety

  • Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.
  • Food supplements should not be used as a substitute for a varied, balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle.
  • If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or have a medical condition, consult a healthcare professional before use.
  • Stop taking a supplement and seek advice if you notice any unwanted effects.
  • Store in a cool, dry place out of reach of children.
  • Always read the ingredients list if you have allergies or intolerances.
