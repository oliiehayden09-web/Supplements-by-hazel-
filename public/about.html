About us

Thoughtfully formulated support for everyday wellbeing

Supplements by Hazel is a UK wellness brand supporting the wellbeing of women and men. Our mission is simple: clean, high-quality supplements that fit into everyday life, so people can make confident, informed choices about their health at every stage.

How we make them

No fads, no confusion

Our formulations come from more than two years of research into vitamins, minerals and botanical extracts, and are designed to complement a balanced diet and healthy lifestyle. Every supplement is made without unnecessary additives, fillers or bulking agents, so each ingredient has a clear purpose.

We combine current nutritional science with a focus on simplicity, transparency and quality, and label everything clearly so you always know exactly what you're taking.

The range supports a variety of needs for women and men: energy, focus and resilience, active living, and everyday wellness.

Meet Hazel · Founder

A trusted voice in men's and women's wellness

Hazel Hayden is a Consultant Nurse and British Menopause Society Registered Specialist and Trainer, with 38 years in the NHS, 19 of them in primary care. She founded the Bristol Menopause Clinic and Bristol Health Hub, where she supports women and men with personalised, evidence-based care.

She began her career as a specialist nurse in cardiovascular and respiratory health. In 2006, after the birth of her two sons, she expanded into women's health: menstrual health, perimenopause, menopause and healthy ageing. Her men's health work covers testosterone health, cardiovascular risk, weight management and lifestyle.

Hazel holds a Master's degree in Sexual Health in Menopause and a Diploma in Nutrition, and trains healthcare professionals across the UK in women's health, men's health and midlife wellness.

A personal mission

Why Hazel made her own

Hazel's own menopause, and decades of listening to the people she supports, led her to create a supplement brand built on three principles: quality, clarity and transparency.

Frustrated by the poor quality and high cost of many supplements, often full of fillers with minimal active ingredients, she spent two years researching and refining clean formulations. The result is the Supplements by Hazel range: thoughtfully made, additive-free supplements for the different needs of women and men.

Our supplements are food supplements, not medicines. They're made with the same attention to detail Hazel brings to all her work, and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
