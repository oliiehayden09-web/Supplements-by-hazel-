FAQs

Is Clarity suitable for vegetarians and vegans?
Yes. Clarity is suitable for both vegetarians and vegans. The capsule and all the ingredients are free from animal-derived products, and we don't use gelatine or other animal-based binders. If you follow a plant-based diet, Clarity can help you get nutrients that can be harder to obtain from food alone, such as vitamin B12 and vitamin D.

How should I take Clarity?
Take one capsule a day, ideally with a meal and a glass of water. Taking it with food helps the absorption of some nutrients and makes it part of your routine. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Clarity can be taken all year round.

Are there any side effects?
Clarity's ingredients are generally well tolerated. As with any food supplement, individual responses vary: some people notice mild digestive changes, particularly if they're new to live cultures. If you notice any unexpected effects, stop taking it and speak to a healthcare professional. Always read the ingredients if you have allergies or intolerances.

Can I take Clarity with other supplements?
Clarity can be taken alongside many other supplements, but check the nutrient content of everything you take so you don't exceed recommended levels, for example if you already take a multivitamin. If you take prescription medication or have a medical condition, speak to your doctor or pharmacist first.
