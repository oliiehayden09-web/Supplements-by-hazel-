FAQs

How should I take Rosy?
Take one capsule in the evening before bed, swallowed with water. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.

Is Rosy suitable for vegetarians or vegans?
Yes. Rosy is suitable for both vegetarians and vegans. The capsule and all the ingredients are free from animal-derived products, and we avoid gelatine, artificial colours and flavours.

Can I take Rosy with other medication or supplements?
If you take prescription medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a medical condition, speak to a healthcare professional before starting Rosy or any new supplement.

Are there any side effects?
Rosy is generally well tolerated, but some people may be sensitive to certain ingredients. If you notice any adverse effects, stop taking it and speak to a healthcare professional.
