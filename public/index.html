Meet Hazel

A trusted voice in midlife wellness

Hazel Hayden is a Consultant Nurse and British Menopause Society Registered Specialist and Trainer, with 38 years in the NHS and nearly two decades focused on midlife health. She founded the Bristol Menopause Clinic and holds a Master's degree in Sexual Health in Menopause and a Diploma in Nutrition.

Her approach is holistic, recognising the connection between lifestyle, nutrition and overall health. She also works in men's health, and trains healthcare professionals across the UK on midlife health for women and men.
