Is Peri suitable for vegetarians or vegans?

Peri is suitable for vegetarians, as it contains no meat, poultry or fish-derived ingredients. It is not suitable for vegans, because some vitamins or botanical carriers may be from non-vegan sources.

Check the full ingredients list on the label to make sure it suits your diet.

How do I take Peri?

Take one capsule twice a day with water, preferably with meals, to help absorption. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.

Who can take Peri?

Peri is designed for adult women in the pre-midlife phase, as part of a healthy, balanced lifestyle. It is not intended for children.

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking prescription medication or have a medical condition, speak to a healthcare professional first.

Are there any side effects?

Peri's ingredients are generally well tolerated, but individual responses vary. If you notice any unwanted effects, such as digestive discomfort or an allergic reaction, stop taking it and speak to a healthcare professional.

Is this a medicine?

Food supplements should not be used as a substitute for a varied, balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or have a medical condition, speak to a healthcare professional before use.
