Cookie Policy

The cookies this website uses, and how to manage them.

Last updated 4 November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Websites can also store information in your browser's local storage, which works in a similar way. This policy covers both, and we call them all "cookies" below.

The cookies we use

This website only uses cookies that it needs to work. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies, and we don't track you across other websites.

Basket and account
Stored in local storage as wix-visitor-…. Keeps your basket, and keeps you logged in to your account. Kept until you clear your browser storage or log out.
Site settings
Stored in local storage as wixSiteScript:…. Lets Wix, which hosts this website, load the site's settings. Short-lived and renewed automatically.
Hosting
A cookie called sec-fetch-unsupported, set by Wix's hosting for security and browser compatibility.

Because these are strictly necessary for the website to work, they don't need your consent.

Other companies

Google Fonts

The fonts on this website load from Google Fonts. To send you the fonts, Google receives your IP address and details of your browser. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies. For more information, see Google's privacy policy.

Wix checkout

When you check out, you move to Wix's secure checkout pages to pay. Those pages use their own cookies, as described in Wix's cookie policy.

If this changes

If we add analytics or advertising cookies in future, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent before using them.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser's settings. If you block them, your basket won't be saved and you won't be able to stay logged in to your account.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, email hello@supplementsbyhazel.com. You can read how we use your personal data in our Privacy Policy.
